# Types of networking
## 1.LAN (LOCAL AREA NETWORK)
LAN (Local Area Network) is a network that connects computers, devices, and other systems within a small geographical area.

[*] ek chhote area mein devices ko connect krta hai.

# EXAMPLE-:
a home, office, school, computer lab, or building.
# ADVANTAGES
.fast speed
.low cost
. easy management

## 2.MAN(METROPOLITAN AREA NETWORK)
MAN is a high speed network that interconnects multiple LOCAL AREA NETWORKS across a town,city,or large campus.
It is larger than a LAN but smaller than a WAN.

[*]ek poore city ke network ko connect karta hai.

# EXAMPLE-:
.Cable tv network
.city wide internet network
# RANGE-:
10-100KM

## WAN(WIDE AREA NETWORK)
WAN is a telecommunications network that extends over a large geographical area.such as cities, states, countries, or even continents.

[*]sabse bada network jo counties aur conyinents ko connects karta hai.

# example-:
.internet
.bank network
# RANGE-:
UNLIMITED

## WLAN(WIRELESS LOCAL AREA NETWORK)
WLAN is a local area network that allows devices to connect and communicate without using any physical wires or cables.

[*] LAN ka wireless version
# EXAMPLE-:
home wifi,collage wifi
# range-:
Indoor: लगभग 20–50 meters
🌳 Outdoor/open area: लगभग 50–100+ meters
📡 Better/high-power equipment mein range 100 meters se kaafi zyada bhi ho sakti hai.

## CAN(CAMPUS AREA NETWORK)
CANis a network that connects multiple LANs within a limited geographical area.
such as a university, college, corporate campus, or organization.

[*] kai local network ko ek hi campus main connect krta hai

# EXAMPLE-:
university campus,large company campus
# RANGE-:
1-10 KM

## PAN(PERSONAL AREA NETWORK)
PAN is a network used to connect personal devices within a very short range around an individual.

[*] bhaut chotta network jo ek hi network ko connect krta hai
# EXAMPLE-:
Laptop + Bluetooth Mouse
# RANGE-:
1-10M
