# WHAT IS TOPOLOGY?
Network Topology is the arrangement or structure of devices such as computers, switches, routers, and servers in a network,
 including how they are connected and how data flows between them.

 [*] Network Topology ka matlab hai network ke andar computers, servers, switches, routers aur other devices kis tarah 
 connected aur arranged hain, aur unke beech data kis tarah flow karta hai.
# EXAMPLE-:
        Internet
           |
         Router
           |
         Switch
        /  |  \
      PC1 PC2 PC3
