# What is Networking?
Computer networking is the process of connecting two or more
computers or devices to communicate and share data and resources.

= Jab 2 ya 2 se zyada devices aapas mein connect hokar data/information exchange karte hain, usse Networking kehte hain.
## Examples
- Computers
- Smartphones
- Servers
- Routers
- Printers
## Benefits

- Data sharing
- Resource sharing
- Communication
- Internet access
